<objective>
Decouple "recommendable" from "has an affiliate link" and turn the existing Apply CTA into an affiliate-signalled link, without adding a disclosure surface.

Three edits against the live 11-card pipeline:
1. Remove the `applyUrl` hard-drop in `loadCards.ts` so a card without a link still loads, ranks, and is recommended (D-06 / AFF-02).
2. Change the CTA `rel` to `sponsored nofollow noopener`, rendered only when a link exists (AFF-01 / D-05). Extract the anchor's href/rel/target decision into a pure, testable helper so the assertion needs no component-render tooling.
3. Prove, by test, that affiliate presence never reorders ranking (D-07 / AFF-02).

Purpose: AFF-01 and AFF-02 (revised). The bilingual affiliate disclosure is intentionally NOT built — it was dropped by product-owner decision (D-08); re-adding it would re-open the requirement.
Output: link-agnostic card loading, an affiliate-signalled CTA, and three Wave-0 tests.
</objective>
